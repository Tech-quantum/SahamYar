SAHAMYAR - PROJECT STATUS
Last documentation update: 2026-11-21

PRODUCT SCOPE
SahamYar is an intelligent analysis assistant for Iran's capital market.
It analyzes the market, instruments, technical and fundamental data,
news and disclosures, and produces reasoned BUY / SELL / HOLD / WAIT
opinions with risks and scenarios.

OUT OF SCOPE
Personal portfolio management, recording transactions, cost-basis or
personal profit/loss calculations, Excel transaction imports, and
automatic order placement are outside the confirmed product scope.

SUPPORTED INSTRUMENT CATEGORIES
- COMMON_STOCK: ordinary shares
- RIGHTS: subscription rights
- FUND: investment funds
- DEBT: debt securities
- OTHER: other or unidentified instruments

TECHNOLOGY
Android: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Retrofit.
Backend: Python, Flask, TSETMC provider, Codal parser/client, pytest.

DATA PRINCIPLES
- Never replace missing data with zero or fabricated values.
- Distinguish source facts, deterministic calculations, and AI interpretation.
- Identify cached data and its timestamp.
- Use explicit statuses: AVAILABLE, PARTIAL, UNAVAILABLE,
  INSUFFICIENT_DATA, UNSUPPORTED, ERROR.

KNOWN BACKEND AREAS
TSETMC: instrument search, market data, prices and daily history.
Codal: financial report discovery, retrieval, parsing and normalization.
Technical indicators: SMA, EMA, RSI and MACD.
Scheduler: must not start as an import side effect.
